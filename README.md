**Technological Sandbox** is a tech modpack that allows factory construction, logistics, automation, and expansion game inspired by other popular industry simulation games as well as Team CoFH's Thermal Series, but with some magic as well as new and untouched horizons to explore!

## The Purpose
Technological Sandbox provides the most popular tech mods combined with some magic and exploration mods to widen your gameplay experience. There are no quests however, but can be played with survival mode whether in normal or peaceful modes for a tycoon experience. Or it can be played with creative mode for a sandbox experience. Getting used to these systems will require a lot of practice, so we recommend playing with friends via the included [World Host](https://modrinth.com/mod/world-host) mod. Your choice, your rules, your freedom.

## FAQ
**Q: What are the system requirements of this modpack?**

A: This modpack requires a modern, beefy PC with at least 8 GB of memory as the minimum and at least 16 GB of the recommend memory. This modpack also requires a system that supports OpenGL 4.6 as well.

**Q: Can I make a derivative of this modpack?**

A: Yes you can, as long as you credit its original creator, Woody Takada, and not change the license as the Apache 2.0 License requires attribution.

**Q: Can I feature this modpack on my video?**

A: Yes you can, as long as you credit by putting the link for this modpack on your video's description.
